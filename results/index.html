How these pages are written: the five rules
  • No client is named. Every engagement here is anonymised. The numbers, the reports and the quotes are real and traceable to a close we performed or a recorded call; the company names are not published. Putting a client's financial story on the internet under their own name is the client's call.
  • No client dollar figures. Percentages, multiples, hours, counts and timeframes only. A client's revenue and cash position are theirs.
  • Quotes are real or absent. Every quote comes from a meeting transcript, with filler words removed and an ellipsis where anything was cut. Nothing was reworded, and nothing was invented. Where a client's words were only paraphrased in our notes, the page summarises in third person rather than inventing a quote.
  • No third-party names. Our clients' clients, staff and contractors stay out of it too.
  • The parts that have not worked are on the page. One of these four is a turnaround still in progress. It is written up as one, in the owner's own words, rather than held back until a better quarter makes it read cleanly.

Questions

The ones people actually ask on the first call

Why are there no client revenue figures on these pages?

Because they belong to the client. Percentages, multiples, hours, counts and timeframes tell you whether something worked without publishing a private company's revenue, profit or cash position.

Why are the clients not named?

Because the story on each page is a financial story. An owner can clear the use of their name and still not want their margin history indexed under it for a decade. The numbers and the reports are the same either way, so anonymising costs you nothing as a reader.

Are these the only clients you have?

No. These are the four engagements with enough documented material to write up honestly. Several other agencies appear nowhere on the site by choice.

Are the quotes edited?

Filler removed, never reworded. We cut the "ums" and the false starts every recorded conversation has, and an ellipsis marks anywhere a phrase was dropped. The words are the client's. Where we only have a paraphrase in our notes, the page summarises in third person rather than inventing a quote.

How long before an engagement shows results like these?

The audit month produces the diagnosis and the 90-day plan. The margin numbers on these pages accumulated over quarters. The one that moved fastest was receivable days: 13 days better inside a single month, once a weekly report and a written follow-up rule existed.
