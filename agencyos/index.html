Here's what four of the frameworks actually do, and why agency owners specifically need them.

Hiring
The Hiring Gate
Most agency owners hire based on how busy they feel. The Hiring Gate replaces that with three conditions: capacity is truly constrained, the model can afford it (cash on hand, profit, and revenue per employee all at benchmark), and the hire is tied to revenue or efficiency. If any one fails, you delay, and you know exactly what has to change first.
Compensation
Owner Pay Framework
Founder pay is one of the most mismanaged line items in small agencies. This framework gives you a defensible number based on your revenue tier, profit margin, and tax obligations, so you stop guessing and stop pulling from the business account hoping it works out.
Team
Revenue Per Employee
One number tells you whether your team size and revenue are in balance. The OS gives you RPE benchmarks by revenue tier and a simple way to track them monthly. When RPE drifts, you know whether the fix is pricing, utilization, or headcount before it shows up in margin.
Taxes
Tax Rate Planning Ranges
Federal, state, and self-employment taxes can stack to 30 to 40% of net income for agency owners. This gives you a planning range by income level so you're reserving the right amount monthly, not scrambling in Q4 or writing a check you weren't ready for.
