Why Fathom sits on top of QuickBooks

QuickBooks Online holds the books: every transaction, reconciliation and journal entry. It is standard with CPAs and buyers, and the rest of the finance stack, payroll and spend cards included, syncs into it. What it doesn't do well is answer an owner's question in five minutes.

Fathom is the reporting layer. It reads directly from QuickBooks Online and turns the closed books into dashboards, KPIs and cash flow forecasts built for owners who aren't accountants. Because it only reads, there is one set of numbers: the dashboard, the tax return and the forecast all start from the same ledger.

The full stack and how it connects is in the agency financial tech stack.

The chart of accounts Fathom depends on

Fathom can only chart what QuickBooks separates. If delivery cost and overhead share an account, no dashboard can show gross margin. These are the splits we set up in QuickBooks before Fathom is connected.

Account groupWhat it separatesWhat it lets Fathom show
Revenue by service lineEach offer in its own income accountRevenue mix, and which line carries the agency
Pass-throughsRebilled media and production, income and costAgency income without inflated revenue
Delivery costTeam and contractors on client workGross profit margin and people cost
OverheadEverything that runs the agencyOperating profit margin and break-even point
Owner payW-2 wages apart from distributionsOwner pay against its benchmark

What a Fathom setup takes, in order

The order is the whole trick. Each step depends on the one before it, and skipping ahead is how most dashboards end up wrong.

  1. 1

    Reconcile QuickBooks

    Every bank, card and processor account matched to its statement through the most recent month.

  2. 2

    Restructure the chart of accounts

    Service lines, pass-throughs, delivery cost, overhead and owner pay each in their own accounts, as in the table above.

  3. 3

    Connect Fathom and map the accounts

    Fathom reads the restructured accounts, so gross margin, people cost and operating profit calculate on the right lines.

  4. 4

    Set targets from your goals

    Targets come from the goals set in the Agency Financial Audit month and the published benchmarks, not from defaults.

  5. 5

    Add the charts you keep asking for

    A service-line share, pass-throughs against their income, margin per project: whatever question comes up every month.

Common Fathom and QuickBooks setup mistakes

  • Connecting Fathom before the chart of accounts is fixed. The dashboard looks finished and shows the wrong margin with total confidence.
  • Pass-throughs counted as revenue. A big media month makes the agency look larger and more profitable than it is.
  • Contractors in overhead. Gross margin reads high, overhead reads bloated, and the people cost chart misses part of the team.
  • Reporting on unreconciled books. Fathom faithfully charts whatever QuickBooks holds, errors included. The close comes first.
  • Default targets. A default target gets ignored. Set targets from your own goals and benchmarks.
  • Sharing the full P&L to share one KPI. Anyone who sees the P&L can work back to owner pay. A report that stops at the gross profit line solves it.

Questions

The ones people actually ask on the first call

Does Fathom work with QuickBooks Online?

Yes. Fathom reads directly from QuickBooks Online and builds dashboards, KPIs and cash flow forecasts from it. QuickBooks stays the single source of truth.

Which KPIs should an agency track in Fathom?

We build six groups: revenue (including mix by service line), profit (gross and operating margin, break-even, margin of safety), people cost, cash and runway, receivables and AR days, and year-to-date comparison against last year and forecast.

What has to be in place in QuickBooks before Fathom?

Reconciled accounts and a chart of accounts that separates revenue by service line, pass-throughs, delivery cost, overhead and owner pay. Without those splits Fathom can't show gross margin or people cost correctly.

How often does the Fathom dashboard update?

With each month-end close. On the Scale tier it is joined by a weekly financial report.

Can Fathom forecast cash flow from QuickBooks?

Yes. Fathom builds cash flow forecasts from the QuickBooks Online data alongside the dashboards, so the forecast starts from the same closed books as the reports.

Do I get my own Fathom login?

Yes. You get your own login, not a PDF. The dashboard is included on Growth and Scale, and is a $200 a month add-on on Starter.
