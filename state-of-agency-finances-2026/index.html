Agency finance questions, answered

What's a good profit margin for a marketing agency?

10 to 25 cents of operating profit on every dollar, rising with size: 10 to 15 under $600K in revenue, 15 to 20 from $600K to $1.2M, and 18 to 25 above $1.2M. See the profit benchmarks by agency size.

How fast are marketing agencies growing in 2026?

The average digital agency grew 7.5% in 2025 (Promethean Research). The typical agency we work with grew 24% from January to September 2026, compared with the same months of 2025.

Is AI cutting agency jobs?

Less than expected. In McKinsey's 2026 survey, 32% of companies expected AI to cut headcount within a year and 14% did. At the agencies we work with, payroll grew 20% while revenue grew 28%.

How much cash should an agency keep in the bank?

Three months of costs, before raising owner pay or adding a salary. Profitable agencies we work with were 4x as likely to hold that much. See how to set owner pay.

When should an agency hire?

Once your profit goal and a 3-month cash reserve have held for a full quarter. A new salary starts on day one, long before the new person pays for themselves.
